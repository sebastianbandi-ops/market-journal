PJM market explorer

Independent project · Sebastian Bandi

I built this dashboard to follow electricity demand, generation and auction activity in PJM, the regional grid operator. These views capture the project on August 13, 2026.

Where did demand surprise us?

The explorer pairs a ranked table with an hourly chart. In the AEP zone, the tooltip shows actual demand running 527 MW above forecast at hour ending 14.

Where is that on the grid?

I layered power plants, substations, transmission lines and gas routes on a map so I could connect a market location with the infrastructure around it.

How has auction activity changed?

I grouped cleared megawatts and record counts by auction year. The final bar includes published monthly files through August, so comparisons with complete years need care.
