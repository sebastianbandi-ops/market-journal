About

From project finance to power markets

Over two summers at Constellation Energy, I studied a power project from two sides. In project finance, I researched financing options and project risks. In origination, I looked at how outages and curtailment affect what a generator can deliver under a power purchase agreement.

At Penn State, my research looks at flexible natural gas pipeline contracts for power generators. I’m interested in what happens when the terms of a fuel contract meet the hourly demands of the grid.
